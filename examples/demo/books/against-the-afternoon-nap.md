# Against the Afternoon Nap

## The Night Is What Counts

Daytime sleep is a symptom, not a cure. If you need a nap, your nights are too short or too broken, and the honest fix is to repair them.

In twenty years of running a sleep clinic, I have seen many patients with insomnia whose trouble began with a daily nap. Once they stopped napping, most fell asleep faster within two weeks.

A cohort study following 3,000 adults for ten years found that people who napped more than an hour a day had higher rates of heart disease. The study could not show that napping caused the disease, but it should give nap enthusiasts pause.

## Better Habits

### Light and Movement

A ten-minute walk in daylight lifts afternoon alertness without touching your sleep pressure. I believe this is the single most underrated habit in modern life.

### When a Nap Is Acceptable

I make one exception. Shift workers and new parents who cannot protect their nights may need a short nap of no more than 20 minutes to stay safe, especially before driving.
